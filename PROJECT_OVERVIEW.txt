WHAT IT IS
----------
A short squeeze scanner that treats "which number is right?" as a first-class
question. Five providers are queried in parallel, merged field by field with an
explicit priority order, and every disagreement is kept and reported rather than
silently resolved. The score says how much of itself the data actually supports.

SCORE
-----
  si         short interest as a share of float
  borrow     days to cover + float scarcity
  liquidity  short position in days of real traded volume
  momentum   52-week compression + rising short position + day change
  flow       FINRA off-exchange short volume + its 5d-vs-20d trend

  Presets (si/borrow/liquidity/momentum/flow):
    classic      .40 .25 .20 .15 .00     the 1.x model, preserved
    balanced     .30 .20 .18 .12 .20     default
    tight_float  .30 .30 .28 .07 .05
    momentum     .22 .13 .15 .35 .15
    flow         .20 .15 .15 .10 .40

  A component with no inputs is DROPPED and the remaining weights are
  renormalised — it is never scored zero. `evidence_weight` reports how much of
  the model ran; `components_missing` lists what was left out.


DATA CONFIDENCE
---------------
  40% coverage + 25% corroboration + 25% agreement + 10% freshness, 0-100.
  A single uncorroborated source scores 0.6 on agreement, not 1.0.
  `confidence_adjusted_score` = squeeze_score × (0.65 + 0.35 × confidence/100).


SOURCES
-------
  finra     daily   off-exchange short volume (free) + consolidated SI (needs creds)
  yahoo     live    float, fundamentals, exchange short interest
  stooq     daily   independent EOD bars; cross-check + backtest data
  sec       qtrly   filed share count (set SEC_CONTACT_EMAIL)
  finviz    daily   float short + days to cover (needs FINVIZ_AUTH_TOKEN)
  snapshot  static  bundled offline sample, never overrides a live value

  A failing source degrades the scan instead of killing it: it records
  error/empty/partial in its health block and the others carry the run.


BACKTEST
--------
  Entry:   score >= threshold AND volume > N x 20-day average, evaluated on
           bar t and FILLED AT BAR t+1's OPEN (no same-bar lookahead)
  Exits:   intrabar take-profit / stop-loss (stop assumed first when a bar
           touches both), optional trailing stop, time exit in TRADING days
  Costs:   commission + slippage in bps, both sides
  Output:  dated equity curve, max drawdown, CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, exposure,
           profit factor, expectancy, per-ticker breakdown, and an equal-weight
           buy-and-hold benchmark over the same window

  LIMITATION: free short-interest history does not exist. Short interest is held
  at its latest value across the window, so only the price/volume/flow
  components vary. Every signal frame carries `si_basis` saying so.

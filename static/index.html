Trades

Not financial advice. Short interest from free providers is stale by construction — exchange files settle twice a month and reach vendors days later. Treat every score as a prompt to look closer, never as a signal.

Sources: Yahoo Finance · Stooq · Finviz · FINRA RegSHO · SEC EDGAR. Backtests are frictionless beyond the modelled commission and slippage, assume fills at the open, and ignore borrow availability and hard-to-borrow fees.
